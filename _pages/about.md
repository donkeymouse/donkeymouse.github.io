<div class="academic-home">
  <p class="lead">
    I am a robotics and computer vision researcher working on thermal infrared perception for robot autonomy. I completed my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering at <a href="https://www.snu.ac.kr">Seoul National University</a>, advised by <a href="https://ayoungk.github.io/">Prof. Ayoung Kim</a> in the <a href="https://rpm.snu.ac.kr">Robust Perception and Mobile Robotics Laboratory</a>.
  </p>

  <p>
    My research focuses on data scaling and radiometric representation learning for deep thermal perception, including thermal object detection, RGB-to-thermal translation, thermal image denoising, place recognition, SLAM-oriented perception, and vision-language prompting for controllable image translation. I also work on underwater robotic monitoring, open-vocabulary perception, image enhancement, and biofouling segmentation for infrastructure inspection.
  </p>
